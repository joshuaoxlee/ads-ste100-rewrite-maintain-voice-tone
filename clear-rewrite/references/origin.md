# Source and adaptation

This skill adapts the workflow and meaning-preservation principles of [danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill](https://github.com/danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill), copyright (c) 2026 Dustin Yuchen Teng, distributed under the MIT License. The original license notice is included in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).

Source revision reviewed: [`32511c6992ecb5f1971e46a2943f2e6adceedafe`](https://github.com/danyuchn/asd-ste100-skill/tree/32511c6992ecb5f1971e46a2943f2e6adceedafe), retrieved on 6 October 2026. Reviewed the main skill, writing-rule reference, before-and-after examples, and structural linter design.

The adaptation retains attention to ambiguity, factual fidelity, modality, and copy-ready output. It changes the audience from agents and technical readers to readers of prose in any genre. Voice preservation is the default. Marketing and persuasive writing receive dedicated guidance. Sentence length, punctuation, tense, vocabulary, and rhetorical devices are contextual choices rather than fixed restrictions.

The upstream linter and examples are not bundled: mechanical style checks would conflict with this skill's treatment of voice, and the examples here are newly written for the broader scope. This package is instruction-only and needs no scripts, credentials, connectors, or network access for rewriting supplied text.

This package does not reproduce the official ASD-STE100 dictionary or standard and does not claim certified compliance or endorsement by ASD or the upstream author.
